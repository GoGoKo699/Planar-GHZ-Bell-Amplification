# Contribution assessment and scientific-scope freeze

**7 October 2026. Decision: retain the consolidated result and freeze scientific scope for contribution review.** This active copy accompanies the authorized initialization of `GoGoKo699/Planar-GHZ-Bell-Amplification`. The original dated assessment is preserved in the archive. This is an author-side assessment, not independent proof review or exhaustive priority clearance.

## The conclusion worth assessing

For any fixed finite family of unbiased binary coplanar qubit measurements, the joint-measurability norm equals the asymptotic full-correlation Bell-amplification factor. A phase-adjusted GHZ state and explicitly generated Bell coefficients attain that factor, within a universal constant of the all-state optimum at every party number.

The physical question is not whether GHZ correlations can be large. It is whether arbitrary given noisy planar detectors can expose every amount of incompatibility, and what normalized exponential rate any state could achieve with those same detectors. The construction answers that question without searching over increasingly complicated states or supplying sharper measurements.

## What changed in the consolidation

The active theorem is now self-contained, with an explicit reading order, definitions of the optimization and its absolute local normalization, exact handling of redundant and collinear families, and attribution inside the proof. It uses the supplied Yoshino et al. primal/dual certificate; its geometry is not presented as a separate discovery.

The all-state upper bound is slightly stronger. Let r be the largest Bloch-vector length and nu the symmetric-hull perimeter divided by four. It is enough to make N-1 sites jointly measurable, leaving one site an arbitrary valid quantum measurement. The previous upper bound nu^N sharpens to r nu^(N-1). This is an application of a known locality observation, not a second theorem to promote as novelty. The exponent remains nu.

For the retained irregular triple, the upper bound rules out a factor-two full-correlation Bell value below twenty parties. The unchanged twenty-five-party construction attains it. This narrows a finite comparison without undertaking an exact-party-number classification.

Two interpretation checks are now explicit. General equatorial Bell operators have complementary-bitstring blocks, so their maximizing states are locally flipped GHZ-type states. That structure alone does not identify a violating functional. Also, the canonical all-zero/all-one GHZ need not maximize an arbitrary fixed functional; a four-term planar CHSH countercontrol demonstrates this. Our particular functional does have its maximum on that canonical block.

For the constructed state all proper equatorial marginal correlators vanish, giving a normalized full distribution with no postselection. That explains why a full-correlation witness is sufficient here. It does not extend the homogeneous all-state bound to arbitrary marginal-containing Bell expressions.

## The strongest predecessor comparisons

- **Yoshino et al., arXiv:2609.38836v1:** the compatibility norm, exact perimeter criterion, redundant-setting reduction, parent construction and optimal dual coefficients are all inherited. Their uploaded text ends with that compatibility proof. The added assertion begins at the Bell conversion and its matching asymptotic rate.
- **Plavala–Guhne–Quintino, arXiv:2403.10564v4:** general qubit incompatibility activation and convergence of Bell-noise thresholds to compatibility are already proved. Their Discussion explicitly asks which state families and party counts suffice. The declared result addresses only the unbiased binary planar subclass; it adds an explicit prescription and a quantitative normalized-rate comparison, not a new general existence theorem. Their positive-map proof route was checked at the relevant construction and conclusion; no complete independent reproduction of its external cone-map theorem is claimed.
- **Werner–Wolf, quant-ph/0102024, Section V.D and VII:** GHZ extremality with optimized two-setting observables is established. Their explicit two-setting restriction prevents simply quoting it as the arbitrary fixed many-setting theorem. Conversely, we must not sell the elementary anti-diagonal structure as a new universal GHZ-optimality principle.
- **Loulidi–Nechita, arXiv:2205.12668, Theorems 8.1–8.2:** compatibility-norm upper bounds are established, in their fixed-Alice bipartite setting. The bound is an input to the connection, not our novelty.
- **Designolle–Vertesi–Pokutta, arXiv:2310.20677, Eqs. (6)–(8):** optimized regular-polygon GHZ constructions can improve finite-party constants. We do not claim a better symmetric Bell facet or better sample complexity. The question here fixes irregular and unequally sharp measurements in advance.

These are primary-text comparisons. New broad searches were largely unhelpful and returned unrelated results; they are not evidence for an absence of prior work. Three requested PDF page screenshots failed with cache-miss responses. No unviewed plot or table datum is used. The prior uploaded-paper visual audit is preserved but was not repeated in this pass.

## Strongest objection, and why the result is retained

The serious objection is that the connection is concise once the compatibility certificate and standard GHZ algebra are supplied. That may limit its perceived scope or significance. It cannot be answered by accumulating more examples, extra digits, a device simulation, or a nominally different carrier.

The positive case is that the added implication converts a local incompatibility classification into a constructive all-size Bell result and an exact operational exponent, for an entire fixed measurement class. It answers a restricted version of an explicitly identified state-family question. Its upper bound compares with every state and full-correlation functional, not a deliberately weak benchmark.

That is sufficient to retain one coherent theoretical project. It is not sufficient to predict editorial acceptance or declare the originality audit complete. The appropriate next step is focused author review of the compact theorem and this comparison in a versioned record, not another automatic extension.

## Stop boundary

Scientific scope is frozen for that assessment. Do not add bias, noncoplanarity, higher-dimensional systems, exact minimum-party classification, arbitrary detector inefficiency, self-testing, genuine multipartite nonlocality or a statistical-complexity theorem merely to make the package appear larger. Such questions may be worthwhile separately, but are not missing premises of the existing claim.
